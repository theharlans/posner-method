# Ty, Inc. v. Publications International Ltd.

- **Citation:** Ty, Inc. v. Publications International Ltd., 292 F.3d 512 (7th Cir. 2002)
- **Court and date:** U.S. Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit, May 30, 2002
- **Author:** Posner
- **Source:** https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/292/512/642473/
- **Topics:** copyright, fair use, remedies

## What the court did

Reversed a judgment for the toy maker: whether photographs of Beanie Babies in collectors' guides were fair use could not be decided on summary judgment, and any recovery would be limited to profits attributable to the photos.

## The method on display

- Fair use treated as an economic question. Copying that complements the original (a review, a guide that helps buyers) tends to be fair; copying that substitutes for the original or for a market the owner would normally exploit tends not to be.
- Remedies tied to causation: the copyright owner recovers profits caused by the infringing material, not all profits of the infringing product.

## Use it for

Fair use, derivative markets, and apportioning infringer's profits.
