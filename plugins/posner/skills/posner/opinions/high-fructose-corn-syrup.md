# In re High Fructose Corn Syrup Antitrust Litigation

- **Citation:** In re High Fructose Corn Syrup Antitrust Litigation, 295 F.3d 651 (7th Cir. 2002)
- **Court and date:** U.S. Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit, 2002 (rehearing denied August 5, 2002)
- **Author:** Posner
- **Source:** https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/295/651/597286/
- **Topics:** antitrust, price fixing, summary judgment, economic evidence

## What the court did

Reversed summary judgment for the defendants. The combined evidence was enough for a jury to infer an express price-fixing agreement.

## The method on display

- Two kinds of evidence: proof that the market is structured so that collusion would pay (few sellers, a standardized product, inelastic demand), and proof of conduct that makes sense only if there was an agreement.
- Evidence is weighed as a whole, not item by item. Pieces that are weak alone can together support an inference of agreement.

## Use it for

Proving or rebutting an alleged agreement among competitors, including standards-setting and licensing coordination claims.
