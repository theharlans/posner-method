# ATA Airlines, Inc. v. Federal Express Corp.

- **Citation:** ATA Airlines, Inc. v. Federal Express Corp., 665 F.3d 882 (7th Cir. 2011)
- **Court and date:** U.S. Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit, December 27, 2011
- **Author:** Posner
- **Source:** https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca7/11-1382/11-1382-2011-12-27-opinion-2011-12-27.html
- **Topics:** contracts, promissory estoppel, damages experts, regression analysis

## What the court did

Reversed a $66 million jury verdict and ordered the case dismissed. The letter agreement lacked an essential price term, promissory estoppel failed, and the damages expert's regression model was unreliable.

## The method on display

- Expert evidence is screened on method. A regression that omits obvious variables or rests on unsupported assumptions should not reach the jury, whatever the expert's credentials.
- The trial judge has a duty to scrutinize the method, not only the conclusion.

## Use it for

Daubert challenges to damages experts, including royalty and lost-profits models in patent and licensing cases.
