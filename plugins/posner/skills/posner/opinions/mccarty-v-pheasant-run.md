# McCarty v. Pheasant Run, Inc.

- **Citation:** McCarty v. Pheasant Run, Inc., 826 F.2d 1554 (7th Cir. 1987)
- **Court and date:** U.S. Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit, July 22, 1987
- **Author:** Posner
- **Source:** https://www.leagle.com/decision/19872380826f2d155412152
- **Topics:** torts, negligence, premises liability, Learned Hand formula

## What the court did

Declined to impose negligence liability on a resort hotel for an assault on a guest by an intruder who entered through a sliding door that was not locked.

## The method on display

- Negligence as a cost-benefit judgment in the tradition of Judge Learned Hand: a defendant is negligent if it failed to take a precaution whose cost was less than the expected harm it would have prevented.
- Ask who could have prevented the harm more cheaply. A guest can check a door at almost no cost.

## Use it for

Negligence and duty-of-care questions, and as the anchor citation for the Hand formula in the method checklist.
