# American Hospital Supply Corp. v. Hospital Products Ltd.

- **Citation:** American Hospital Supply Corp. v. Hospital Products Ltd., 780 F.2d 589 (7th Cir. 1986)
- **Court and date:** U.S. Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit, 1986 (Swygert, J., dissenting)
- **Author:** Posner
- **Source:** https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F2/780/589/148335/
- **Topics:** preliminary injunctions, remedies, distribution contracts

## What the court did

Affirmed a preliminary injunction that kept a distribution contract in force pending trial.

## The method on display

- The preliminary injunction standard expressed as a formula: grant the injunction only if P x Hp > (1 - P) x Hd. P is the probability the plaintiff will win on the merits, Hp the irreparable harm to the plaintiff if relief is denied, and Hd the irreparable harm to the defendant if relief is wrongly granted.
- The formula does not replace judgment; it organizes it. Both kinds of error (wrongly granting and wrongly denying) are weighed by their likelihood and cost.

## Use it for

Any request for interim relief: preliminary injunctions in patent, trade secret, or contract cases, and anti-suit injunctions in FRAND disputes.
